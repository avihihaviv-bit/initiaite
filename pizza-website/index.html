The Fire

We didn't build a menu. We built an oven, and let it decide.

Every pizzaiolo will tell you the same thing: the oven does most of the work. Ours burns oak and almond wood, nothing else, and it runs hot enough that a pie is done before you'd finish reading this sentence twice.

That heat is the whole reason the crust comes out the way it does: blistered, a little charred at the high points, chewy enough to hold real weight without going limp. We didn't design that. The fire did.
