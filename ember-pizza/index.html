05 · Questions

What people ask before ordering

Does it actually arrive hot?

Yes. We fire your pizza after you order, not before, and it travels in a vented box built to stop steam from softening the crust. Most orders arrive within 25 minutes of leaving the oven.

Why does it cost more than chain delivery?

Oak-fired ovens, 24-hour dough, and San Marzano tomatoes cost more than a heat lamp and a bag of pre-shredded cheese. You're paying for the fire, not the marketing.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Margherita and Funghi are both vegetarian. Ask and we'll leave off any topping.

Do you do pickup?

Yes, and it's the fastest way to get it hot. Order ahead and it'll be timed to come out of the oven when you walk in.
